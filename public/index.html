📖 Game rules

Teen Patti: Three cards each. Ranking: Trail, Pure Sequence, Sequence, Color, Pair, High Card. Choose blind, seen, chaal, raise, pack or show when two players remain.

Kali Teedi: Spades are trump. Bid for tricks, follow the lead suit, and win tricks with the highest trump or highest card of the lead suit.

Kachuful: Trump suit varies by round. Predict your tricks, follow suit, and earn bonus points for matching your bid.

All chips are virtual, with no real-money betting.
